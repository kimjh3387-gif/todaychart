Today's chart, every day.

todaychart is a one-person YouTube channel (@chart_today) that turns public music charts into a 15-second Top 10 Short every day, and keeps a matching playlist of official music videos up to date.

What the todaychart app does

The todaychart app is a private desktop tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer once a day. It is not offered to other users.

  1. Reads public chart data: the Melon daily chart (Korea) and the Apple Music Top 100 feeds for the USA, Vietnam, Thailand, Singapore and Malaysia.
  2. Renders an original vertical video (our own design, our own synthesized background music) showing the day's Top 10 and highlights.
  3. Uses the YouTube Data API to upload that video to the owner's channel with a scheduled publish time, title, description and tags.
  4. Uses the YouTube Data API to keep the channel's own playlists ("Top 50 M/V", "Top 50 Audio") in chart order by adding and removing videos.
