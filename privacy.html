Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how the todaychart app (the "App") handles data. The App is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel todaychart (@chart_today) to publish that channel's own content. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through YouTube API Services.

What data the App accesses

  • OAuth access to the owner's own YouTube channel — used only to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time, and to create and update the channel's own playlists.
  • Public video search results — titles, channel names, durations and view counts of public videos, used only to pick the official music video of a song for the channel's playlists.

The App does not access, collect or store data about any viewer or any other YouTube user: no names, emails, comments, subscriber lists or analytics of other people.

How data is stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared or uploaded anywhere else.
  • The App keeps a small local cache (song → chosen video ID, checked date) so it does not search for the same song every day. Cached items are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the App deletes its local token on the next run.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.
